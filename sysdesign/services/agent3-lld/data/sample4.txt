FR1: The system shall allow administrators to configure a parking lot with multiple floors.

FR2: The system shall allow the system to manage parking spots on each parking floor.

FR3: The system shall support Car, Truck, and Motorcycle vehicle types.

FR4: The system shall support Compact, Large, and Motorcycle parking spot types.

FR5: The system shall validate parking spot compatibility with the vehicle type.

FR6: The system shall automatically find the nearest available compatible parking spot for an incoming vehicle.

FR7: The system shall reserve an available parking spot when a vehicle enters the parking lot.

FR8: The system shall prevent a parking spot from being assigned to multiple vehicles simultaneously.

FR9: The system shall generate a unique parking ticket when a vehicle is successfully parked.

FR10: The system shall record the vehicle, parking spot, entry timestamp, and ticket status for each parking session.

FR11: The system shall allow the system to retrieve an active parking ticket using its unique ticket ID.

FR12: The system shall calculate the parking fee based on the vehicle's parking duration.

FR13: The system shall support Cash and Card payment methods.

FR14: The system shall process payment for an active parking ticket when a vehicle exits the parking lot.

FR15: The system shall record payment details and payment status for each completed payment.

FR16: The system shall release the occupied parking spot after successful payment.

FR17: The system shall update the parking ticket status after successful payment and vehicle exit.

FR18: The system shall keep the parking spot occupied when payment processing fails.

FR19: The system shall prevent vehicles from being assigned to unavailable or out-of-service parking spots.

FR20: The system shall allow the system to track the availability status of parking spots in real time.


NFR1: The system shall ensure thread-safe parking spot allocation so that the same parking spot cannot be assigned to multiple vehicles concurrently.

NFR2: The system shall complete parking spot lookup and reservation operations within 100 milliseconds under normal operating conditions.

NFR3: The system shall generate parking tickets within 100 milliseconds under normal operating conditions.

NFR4: The system shall use a layered architecture consisting of Controller, Service, Repository, and Model layers.

NFR5: The system shall use PostgreSQL as the relational database.

NFR6: The system shall follow SOLID principles to support maintainability and extensibility.

NFR7: The system shall allow new vehicle types to be added without modifying the core parking allocation workflow.

NFR8: The system shall allow new payment methods to be added without modifying the core payment workflow.

NFR9: The system shall allow new pricing strategies to be introduced without modifying the core payment service.

NFR10: The system shall maintain consistent vehicle, parking spot, ticket, and payment information across all system operations.

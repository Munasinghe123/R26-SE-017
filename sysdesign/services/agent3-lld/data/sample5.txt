FR1: The system shall allow patients to register accounts.

FR2: The system shall allow patients to book appointments with doctors.

FR3: The system shall allow doctors to manage appointments.

FR4: The system shall store patient medical records.

FR5: The system shall allow doctors to create prescriptions.

FR6: The system shall allow patients to make payments.

FR7: The system shall generate bills for treatments.

FR8: The system shall manage departments and doctors.

FR9: The system shall manage laboratory tests.

FR10: The system shall manage pharmacy inventory.

FR11: The system shall allow administrators to manage hospital staff.

SYSTEM: E-Commerce Platform

ARCHITECTURE:
Microservice Architecture

SERVICES:
- Auth Service
- Product Service
- Cart Service
- Order Service
- Payment Service
- Inventory Service
- Shipment Service
- Notification Service

ACTORS:
- Customer
- Seller
- Admin
- Payment Gateway
- Delivery Service

CORE WORKFLOWS:

WORKFLOW: Place Order
1. Customer adds products to cart.
2. Cart Service retrieves cart items.
3. Inventory Service validates stock.
4. Customer applies coupon.
5. Order Service calculates total.
6. Payment Service sends payment request.
7. Payment Gateway validates payment.
8. Payment Gateway returns success/failure.
9. Order Service creates order.
10. Inventory Service reduces stock.
11. Shipment Service creates shipment.
12. Notification Service sends confirmation.

ALTERNATIVE FLOW: Payment Failure
1. Payment Gateway rejects payment.
2. Payment Service marks payment failed.
3. Notification Service sends failure notification.

WORKFLOW: Refund Request
1. Customer submits refund request.
2. Refund Service validates eligibility.
3. Admin approves refund.
4. Payment Gateway processes refund.
5. Notification Service sends refund confirmation.

BUSINESS RULES:
- Orders require available stock.
- Payments must succeed before order creation.
- Refunds allowed within 7 days.
- Customers can review purchased products only.

ORDER STATES:
- Pending
- Paid
- Shipped
- Delivered
- Cancelled
- Refunded

ENTITIES:

User(user_id, email, password, role)

Customer(customer_id, user_id, full_name)

Seller(seller_id, user_id, business_name)

Product(product_id, seller_id, category_id, name, price, stock_quantity)

Category(category_id, name)

Cart(cart_id, customer_id)

CartItem(cart_item_id, cart_id, product_id, quantity)

Order(order_id, customer_id, total_amount, order_status)

OrderItem(order_item_id, order_id, product_id, quantity, price)

Payment(payment_id, order_id, payment_method, payment_status)

Shipment(shipment_id, order_id, tracking_number, shipment_status)

Review(review_id, customer_id, product_id, rating)

Refund(refund_id, order_id, refund_status)

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Customer places many Orders
- Order contains many OrderItems
- Seller manages many Products
- Product belongs to Category
- Order has one Payment
- Order has one Shipment
- Product has many Reviews

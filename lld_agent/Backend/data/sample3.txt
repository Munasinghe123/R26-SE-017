SYSTEM: Smart Hospital Management System

ARCHITECTURE:
Microservice Architecture

SERVICES:

* Patient Service
* Appointment Service
* Billing Service
* Pharmacy Service
* Laboratory Service
* Notification Service

ACTORS:

* Patient
* Doctor
* Admin
* Pharmacist
* Lab Technician

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS:
FR1: Patients can register and login.
FR2: Patients can book appointments.
FR3: Doctors can manage appointments.
FR4: System stores medical records.
FR5: Doctors create prescriptions.
FR6: Patients make payments.
FR7: System generates bills.
FR8: Admin manages departments and staff.
FR9: Lab technicians manage lab tests.
FR10: Pharmacists manage medicine inventory.

WORKFLOW: Appointment Booking

1. Patient logs into system.
2. Patient selects doctor.
3. Appointment Service checks availability.
4. Doctor confirms appointment.
5. Notification Service sends confirmation.

WORKFLOW: Prescription Process

1. Doctor views patient record.
2. Doctor creates prescription.
3. Pharmacy Service checks medicine stock.
4. Pharmacist dispenses medicine.
5. Notification Service sends update.

WORKFLOW: Payment Process

1. Patient views bill.
2. Billing Service calculates payment.
3. Patient makes payment.
4. Billing Service confirms payment.
5. Notification Service sends receipt.

BUSINESS RULES:

* Appointment requires doctor availability.
* Payment must complete before billing confirmation.
* Prescriptions require valid medical records.
* Medicine stock must be available before dispensing.

ENTITIES:

Patient(patient_id, name, email, contact)

Doctor(doctor_id, name, specialization)

Appointment(appointment_id, patient_id, doctor_id, date, status)

MedicalRecord(record_id, patient_id, diagnosis, treatment)

Prescription(prescription_id, patient_id, doctor_id, medicine)

Bill(bill_id, patient_id, amount, payment_status)

Payment(payment_id, bill_id, payment_method)

LabTest(test_id, patient_id, result)

PharmacyInventory(item_id, medicine_name, stock_quantity)

Department(department_id, department_name)

RELATIONSHIPS:

* Patient books many Appointments
* Doctor manages many Appointments
* Patient has many MedicalRecords
* Doctor creates many Prescriptions
* Patient receives many Bills
* Bill has one Payment
* Patient has many LabTests
* Department has many Doctors
